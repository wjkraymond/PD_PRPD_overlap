What Happens When 2 PRPD Sources Overlap by ~50%?

In isolated modes (PRPD 1 Only or PRPD 2 Only), local bin repetition counts are moderate, displaying cool Blues, Cyans, and Greens. When selecting Both, the pulses falling into the ~50% shared space (60°–85° & 320–550 pC) sum together. The repetition count n in those overlapping bins nearly doubles, causing the 3D bars to rise into tall columns and shifting their color directly into vibrant Yellows and Hot Reds in both 2D and 3D.
